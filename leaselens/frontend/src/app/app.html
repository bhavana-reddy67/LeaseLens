Spot risky clauses before you sign

LeaseLens reads rental agreements, employment contracts, loan agreements and other contracts, highlights clauses worth a closer look, explains why, and helps you ask the right questions.

  1. Choose the AI. Local Gemma through Ollama by default, so documents stay on this computer.
  2. Upload a PDF or photo. LeaseLens extracts the text, and you check it.
  3. Review findings. Keyword rules and the AI each assess clauses, with sources shown.
  4. Draft an email about the findings you choose. You edit and send it yourself.

Informational only. LeaseLens is not a lawyer and does not give legal advice.
